## Logo files

- [Wordmark, for light backgrounds](/brand/logo-wordmark.svg)
- [Wordmark, for night backgrounds](/brand/logo-wordmark-on-night.svg)
- [App mark](/brand/logo-mark.svg)

The name is one word, always lowercase: **parascene**. Not ParaScene, not Para Scene, not PARASCENE.

The wordmark is custom lettering. Do not redraw it in a font. The middle of the word is lighter on purpose. Leave that as it is.

The app mark is the violet tile with a white p. Use it for favicons, avatars, and other square crops. Do not set the wordmark inside the tile.

## Colours and type

- **Night** `#0F0D1A` is the public background.
- **Paper** `#F6F7FB` is the light background.
- **Violet** `#7C3AED` is the accent on paper.
- **Signal** `#05C76F` is the accent on night, including the public Enter button.

Text that is not the logo is [Geist](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Geist).

## What to say

parascene is a community for creating and sharing with generative tools.

Models are tools. They do not decide what is art, and neither do we. parascene is not an AI-first platform. Do not call it an AI art site, an AI-only app, or a judge of what counts as real art.
